DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 6, 2026. Applies to the DRAFT 2.16 beta app and this website.

Summary

DRAFT runs on your PC. It has no user accounts, no server receiving your data, no ads and no telemetry. What you import from Riot stays on your computer.

1. Data stored by the app

Everything is saved in the %LOCALAPPDATA%\DraftMid folder on your PC (the name comes from the previous version):

  • Profiles and settings: your champions per role, your preferences and your draft journal.
  • Riot history, if you import it: your Riot ID and, for each of your ranked games, its ID, date, patch, duration, your champion, your position, the result, your K/D/A and the enemy champions. No name or identifier of any other player.
  • Aggregated statistics, if you compute them: anonymous counters per champion and role, and the IDs of matches already read.
  • Technical log: a limited diagnostic file, with no key and no API response.

2. Riot API key

In the beta, imports and computations use a key you enter yourself at the time of the operation. It stays in memory for the duration of the operation, is sent only to Riot in the request header, and is never stored or logged. Never share it.

3. Network connections made by the app

  • Data Dragon (Riot Games): champion artwork.
  • Riot Games API: only when you start an import or a computation.
  • League of Legends client: only if you enable client reading, locally on your PC and read-only. Disabled by default.
  • External links: opened only when you click them.

No data is sent to DRAFT or to any third party.

4. This website

This website is static: no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. The host (Vercel) technically processes IP addresses and access logs to serve and protect the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts that service.

5. Deleting your data

Quit DRAFT, then delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\DraftMid folder. This removes your profiles, imported history and statistics. Exporting your profiles from the settings lets you keep a copy first.

6. Contact

For any question about your data: lucas.mettetal2@gmail.com. You can also open a discussion on GitHub, without posting any key or personal data.
