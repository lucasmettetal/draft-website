01 / THE PROBLEM

A few seconds to decide, five opponents to read.

In champion select you watch the enemy team come together, but you have little time to work out which of your champions handles it best. DRAFT does that reading with you, using explicit rules.

01Read

Engage, divers, crowd control, squishy targets: the enemy team profile at a glance.

02Compare

Every champion in your pool is ranked, with an alternative and what separates them.

03Understand

A game plan for each phase and tips matched to your level, so you learn while playing.

02 / FEATURES

What DRAFT does, with real screenshots.

All images come from the app, with a simulated draft and fictitious data. The interface is currently in French.

CHAMP SELECT PANELS

Next to the client, during the draft

Compact panels show the essentials: enemy composition, recommendation and game plan. They sit around the client without covering it.

ROLE PROFILES

One pool for each of the five roles

Top, Jungle, Mid, ADC, Support: each role keeps its own champions. Switch roles in one click and export your profiles to a file.

GAME PLAN

How to win with this pick

The win condition, threats to watch, targets to punish and useful bans, then tips for laning, mid game and teamfights.

ANALYSIS

The reasoning behind the pick

Criterion by criterion, DRAFT shows what puts one champion ahead of another. When two picks are very close, it says so instead of choosing at random.

GAMES · OPTIONAL

Your experience, next to the advice

If you import your ranked history, DRAFT shows your most played champions. These numbers inform you; they never change the strategic ranking.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, and the decision stays yours.

01

Launch DRAFT

An icon appears next to the Windows clock. No account and no key are needed to start.

02

Set up your pool

In settings, add the champions you play for each role. They are saved on your PC.

03

Enter the enemy picks

Type each enemy champion as it is picked. DRAFT guesses its role, which you can correct in one click.

04

Read the advice, then pick

One champion, an alternative, the reasons and a game plan. You select and lock in yourself, in the client.

04 / RIOT GAMES API

What DRAFT asks Riot for, and why.

DRAFT only uses the official Riot Games API. The core recommendation works without it: the API provides complementary statistics, always triggered by the user.

USE 1 · PERSONAL HISTORY

Import your ranked games

You enter your Riot ID and start the import. DRAFT looks up your account, then reads your ranked solo/duo games.

  • ACCOUNT-V1 /riot/account/v1/accounts/by-riot-id
  • MATCH-V5 /lol/match/v5/matches/by-puuid/{puuid}/ids?queue=420
  • MATCH-V5 /lol/match/v5/matches/{matchId}

Kept, on your PC only: match ID and date, patch, duration, your champion, your position, the result, your K/D/A and the enemy champions. No name or identifier of any other player.

USE 2 · AGGREGATED STATISTICS

Roles and matchups per champion

To guess an enemy champion's role and measure your pool's matchup records, DRAFT counts roles played and results over recent ranked games.

  • LEAGUE-EXP-V4 /lol/league-exp/v4/entries — starting point, random players
  • MATCH-V5 ranked solo/duo match lists and details

Kept: only anonymous counters (champion, role, wins, games) and the IDs of matches already read, so they are never requested twice. No player is stored, displayed or ranked.

Key never exposed

The key is sent in the X-Riot-Token header, never in a URL. It is neither stored nor logged.

Rate limits respected

Requests are spaced out. On a 429 response, DRAFT waits for the Retry-After delay before resuming, without losing what was already read.

No duplicates

Matches already read are never requested again, and the next import resumes where the previous one stopped.

WITH A PRODUCTION KEY

In the beta, each tester runs these computations with their own development key. With a production key, aggregated statistics would be computed once, on a DRAFT server that keeps the key, then distributed as anonymous counters. The app would no longer need any key, and the total number of requests sent to Riot would drop sharply.

05 / RULES AND PRIVACY

An assistant, not a bot.

DRAFT is designed to follow Riot Games' policies for third-party tools: it helps you think, it does not play for you and it uses no hidden information.

Your data stays on your PC. No DRAFT account, no ads, no telemetry.
