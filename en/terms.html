1. The service

DRAFT is a free, independent Windows app that ranks a League of Legends player's champions against the enemy composition and explains each option. It is in beta: version 2.16.0 is available as a public beta on GitHub, and features may change between versions.

2. Nature of the advice

DRAFT's advice relies on explicit rules, hand-written champion sheets and imperfect statistics. It is an opinion, not a guarantee of victory. The player alone decides.

3. Fair use

DRAFT never picks, bans or locks in a champion and takes no in-game action. It must not be modified to do so. You agree to use DRAFT in accordance with Riot Games' terms of service and its policies for third-party tools, and to import only your own accounts' history.
